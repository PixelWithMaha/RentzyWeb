# User Stories

1. As a tenant who has finalized my residency, I want an automated prompt to appear on my "Completed Booking" history so that I can submit experience data instantly.
2. As a prospective client evaluating property listings, I want global review metrics dynamically aggregated beneath property descriptions so that I can weigh risks realistically.
3. As a logged-in visitor inspecting a specific unit, I want to read chronological, individual tenant comments with clear, readable timestamps to gauge historical property state.
4. As a reviewer, I want a user-friendly star widget to map numerical satisfactions to my review quickly.
5. As a reviewer who made a mistake, I want an "Edit" capability to revise previous sentiments securely.
6. As a reviewer whose sentiment has evolved or resolved, I want a "Delete" button to withdraw past public assertions.
7. As a landlord viewing my portfolio dashboard, I want real-time read access to feedback aimed at my specific dwellings to evaluate operation viability.
8. As an active system Administrator, I require the power to override, conceal, or purge offensive and spurious tenant comments ensuring total ecosystem hygiene.
9. As the security core, I must proactively deny feedback attempts from unauthenticated users or entities lacking confirmed `StatusId = 4` transactional signatures on the specific asset target.
